# Ranked creator cold-DM list

Research date: 2026-08-13

Scope: additive prospecting research only. **No outreach has been sent.**

Positioning used: education first; sell the same bounded outcome for a few cents per run, without claiming Omo replaces a competitor's LMS, live classroom, roster, analytics, or free tier.

## How to use this list

- Rank favors: (1) demonstrated coverage of Diffit, MagicSchool, Twee, Formative, or Kahoot; (2) teacher/education audience; (3) general AI-tool reviewers.
- `NEEDS VERIFICATION` means the exact handle or a genuinely viral post URL was not independently confirmed in indexed web results. It is deliberately not guessed.
- A verified channel or creator is not the same thing as a verified viral post. Most post fields remain unfilled for that reason.
- Before any future DM, re-check recent activity, contact policy, sponsorship conflicts, and whether the creator still covers the stated niche. Harry's approval is required before outreach.
