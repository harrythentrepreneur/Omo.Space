# Educator review protocol: first three proof-page skills

## Reviewer and setup

Reviewer: **[Full name]**, **[credential]**, **[role and school/organization]**.
Use one named educator with relevant phonics/early-literacy experience; fill in
the identity before sampling. The proof page stays unpublished until the review
is signed and all evidence is archived.

## Sampling

1. Set **N = 20 independent runs per skill** (60 runs total).
2. Prepare 20 fixed input cards per skill covering the stated graphemes,
   phonemes, digraphs, word positions, scope-and-sequence levels, and target age.
3. Run each card once with the production candidate, recording the exact input,
   settings, workflow version, date/time, run ID, and result link.
4. Save the raw output before editing or interpretation. The educator scores the
   complete output and every listed word, sentence, or marked digraph.

## Scoring rubric

Score each criterion **Pass**, **Minor issue**, or **Critical error**. A run is
error-free only when every requested item passes every applicable criterion.

### phonics-list-generator

- Grapheme-phoneme accuracy: each word matches the requested sound-spelling
  relationship and contains no incorrect segmentation.
- Scope-and-sequence alignment: only taught patterns/phonemes appear, unless the
  prompt explicitly permits an exception.
- Word validity and age-appropriateness: real, classroom-suitable words at the
  requested level; no confusing or needlessly obscure choices.
- Completeness and labeling: requested count, pattern, and answer information are
  present and unambiguous.

### decodable-sentence-creator

- Decodability: sentence words follow the requested correspondences and approved
  high-frequency/exception-word list; untaught patterns are not hidden.
- Grapheme-phoneme accuracy and scope-and-sequence alignment are correct for the
  target level.
- Age-appropriateness and meaning: sentences are grammatical, understandable,
  classroom-suitable, and not misleading.
- Completeness: requested number, target pattern, and any exception labels are
  present.

### digraph-spotter

- No mislabeled digraphs: every marked target is the requested digraph, and a
  digraph is not split into two unrelated sounds or letters.
- Grapheme-phoneme accuracy: the word and marked location support the target
  sound as specified.
- Scope-and-sequence alignment, word validity, and age-appropriateness pass.
- Presentation is unambiguous: directions, markings, and answer key agree.

## Decision gate

Publish a proof page only if **at least 19 of 20 runs per skill (>=95%) are
error-free**, every critical criterion has 100% accuracy, and the educator signs
off on all three skills. Any critical error in a run fails that skill; a failed
skill requires correction and a fresh 20-run sample before publication.

## Evidence to retain

- Dated run IDs/links, exact inputs/settings, workflow versions, and raw outputs.
- Completed scoring sheet with per-run and per-criterion results and any fixes.
- Educator’s full name, credential, role/organization, review date, and signed
  statement: “I reviewed the recorded sample for these three skills and, under
  the criteria above, [approve/do not approve] publication of the proof page.”
- Archive the evidence bundle and proof-page decision together; do not publish
  until the bundle is reproducible from the recorded run IDs.
