# Omo Education SaaS Kill List

Research snapshot: **2026-08-13**. Internal strategy only. Omo should copy the
customer outcome—not a competitor's brand, interface, templates, wording, or
outputs. Every proposed listing below is first-party and starts from the
buyer's own prompt or source material.

## How the ranking works

`priority score = (estimated contribution margin per run × viral score) ÷ difficulty`

- Viral score is 1–5: 40% price shock, 25% visible before/after, 20%
  competitor recognition, and 15% frequency of the teacher pain.
- Difficulty divisor: Low = 1, Medium = 2, High = 4. “High” is reserved
  for stateful tutoring, student data, or judgment-heavy grading.
- Margin is Omo price less rough model, rendering, and storage cost. It excludes
  fixed engineering, payment fees on wallet top-ups, support, refunds, and tax.
- Prices are USD. Monthly equivalents of annual plans are arithmetic comparisons,
  not claims that the competitor permits monthly billing.

## Guardrails for the campaign

- Attack a named, reproducible **feature outcome**, not the whole platform. A
  generated quiz is not equivalent to an LMS, roster, analytics dashboard, or
  live multiplayer infrastructure.
- Put the competitor tier and billing period on screen. For annual-only plans,
  say both the monthly equivalent and the annual charge.
- Do not run a price-attack reel against a free teacher tier. The `$0` and
  quote-only rows remain landscape coverage and later workflow opportunities;
  they are intentionally ranked low.
- Use only Omo-owned prompts, schemas, layouts, evaluation rubrics, and sample
  outputs. Never ingest or mimic competitor templates or examples.
- Re-check the linked price immediately before recording or posting every reel.
