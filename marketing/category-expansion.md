# Omo category expansion after Education

Research cut: 2026-08-13. **GROUNDED** = supported by the linked source (or the supplied Omo brief); **REASONED** = judgment or proposed test. Prices are USD unless shown otherwise.

## Executive call

- **REASONED:** Expand in this order: **1 Education, 2 E-commerce, 3 Real-estate listing media, 4 Short-form content repurposing, 5 Recruiting & career documents.**
- **REASONED:** The best evidence is not generic interest in AI; it is incumbents charging per product, image, credit, candidate action, or a subscription for the exact bounded result.
- **REASONED:** Proposed Omo prices below are launch tests, not verified unit economics. Image/video runs require a cost and retry audit before activation.
- **REASONED:** Exact public keyword volumes were not verified, so no search-volume figures are reported. Paid product pages are the demand proxy.

## 1. Education — defend and deepen the wedge

- **GROUNDED:** Omo already has PhonicsMaker and an approximately 4,500-teacher email audience; current simple phonics workflows sell for about $0.10/run (supplied Omo brief).
- **GROUNDED:** Teachers currently pay $12.99/month for MagicSchool Plus; its named results include quizzes, worksheets, and lesson plans ([MagicSchool pricing](https://www.magicschool.ai/pricing)). Formative charges $20.75/month per teacher, billed annually, for lessons, assessments, practice sets, grading, and feedback ([Formative plans](https://www.formative.com/plans)). Kahoot school tiers run from $3 to $19/month, billed annually, and include quiz generation and PDF question extraction ([Kahoot plans](https://kahoot.com/schools/plans/)).
- **REASONED — buyer/frequency:** Classroom teachers, tutors, interventionists, and homeschool educators; 2–10 runs/week during planning, differentiation, and assessment cycles.

| Workflow | Input → result | Omo price |
|---|---|---:|
| **REASONED — Decodable Sentence Set** | target graphemes, known code, age, count → controlled sentences + QA report | $0.10/run |
| **REASONED — Phonics Word List** | grapheme/phoneme, position, exclusions → grouped word list + exceptions | $0.10/run |
| **REASONED — Differentiated Reading Pack** | passage/topic + 3 reading levels → leveled passages, vocabulary, questions | $0.30/run |
| **REASONED — PDF-to-Exit-Ticket** | source PDF, grade, question mix → answer-keyed quiz + printable result | $0.25/run |
| **REASONED — Printable Worksheet** | skill, examples, difficulty, layout → classroom-ready PDF + answer key | $0.40/run |

- **REASONED — creator/affiliate angle:** Teacher newsletter writers, literacy coaches, curriculum YouTubers, and classroom-resource sellers already demonstrate these outcomes and can link to a one-run proof page.
- **REASONED — risk:** Pedagogical errors, weak decodability controls, student privacy, curriculum mismatch, and print-layout QA. Keep inputs student-anonymous and make answer keys inspectable.

### E-commerce recommendation

- **REASONED:** Ship first: Shopify/Etsy SEO Listing, Amazon Listing Pack, and UGC Script & Shot List. They are text-first, inspectable, low-support, and need no store credentials.
- **REASONED:** Add photo packs only after measuring generation cost, rejection rate, and retry support. Add competitor research only with buyer-supplied URLs or approved data sources.
- **REASONED — creator/affiliate angle:** Amazon FBA educators, Etsy/Shopify YouTubers, e-commerce SEO consultants, product photographers, UGC educators, and email specialists can demonstrate a before/after result and earn on referred runs.
- **REASONED — risk:** Crowded copy tools, platform field/policy changes, invented claims, copyrighted inputs, visual inconsistency, scraping restrictions, large-catalog support, and requests for live store publishing. Keep the initial result downloadable and require merchant approval before use.

## 3. Real-estate listing media

- **GROUNDED:** BoxBrownie prices discrete results directly: image enhancement $1.60, day-to-dusk $4, virtual staging $24, floor-plan redraws from $24, and property copy from $28 for 150 words ([BoxBrownie pricing](https://www.boxbrownie.com/pricing)).
- **GROUNDED:** Virtual Staging AI displays effective annual rates of $0.53–$2.67/photo depending on tier ([Virtual Staging AI pricing](https://www.virtualstagingai.app/prices)). ListingAI charges $19/month for descriptions/social presence and $36/month for video, staging, and CMA reports ([ListingAI pricing](https://www.listingai.co/pricing)).
- **REASONED — buyer/frequency:** Realtors, broker teams, property photographers, and vacation-rental managers; 1–30 listings/month with multiple results per listing.

| Workflow | Input → result | Omo price |
|---|---|---:|
| **REASONED — MLS Description Pack** | verified property facts + voice + field limits → MLS copy, portal copy, fact checklist | $0.50/run |
| **REASONED — Listing Launch Kit** | property facts/photos + audience → captions, email, flyer copy, open-house posts | $1.00/run |
| **REASONED — Photo Enhancement** | room/exterior photo + portal spec → corrected, listing-ready image | $1.00/image |
| **REASONED — Virtual Stage** | empty-room photo + style + disclosure label → staged image + original comparison | $3.00/image |
| **REASONED — Floor-plan Redraw** | legible sketch + dimensions → clean labeled plan + uncertainty flags | $4.00/run |

- **REASONED — creator/affiliate angle:** Real-estate coaches, listing photographers, staging educators, brokerage trainers, and property-marketing channels can sell a visible before/after result.
- **REASONED — risk:** Fair-housing language, inaccurate property facts or geometry, local MLS limits, missing image-edit disclosure, subjective staging, and expensive revisions. Do not estimate value or invent amenities; preserve originals and label edits.

Castmagic starts at $19/month billed annually and turns one media file into transcripts, clips, posts, show notes, and other content assets ([Castmagic pricing](https://www.castmagic.io/pricing)).
- **REASONED — buyer/frequency:** Podcasters, YouTubers, coaches, webinar teams, and small content studios; every episode/video, commonly weekly.

| Workflow | Input → result | Omo price |
|---|---|---:|
| **REASONED — Clip Finder** | long video + audience + clip length → ranked timecodes, hooks, clip briefs | $0.60/run |
| **REASONED — Five Finished Shorts** | long video + brand template → 5 captioned vertical clips + titles | $3.00/run |
| **REASONED — Episode Content Pack** | audio/video + voice → transcript, show notes, 10 posts, email draft | $1.25/run |
| **REASONED — Caption & Chapter Pack** | media + language/style → SRT/VTT, chapters, cleanup report | $0.50/run |

- **REASONED — creator/affiliate angle:** Video-editing educators, podcast coaches, YouTube growth channels, and content-studio operators can publish side-by-side clip breakdowns and refer recurring runs.
- **REASONED — risk:** Large uploads, render cost, copyright/music rights, weak clip selection, caption timing, brand-template support, and highly subjective revisions. Lead with transcript/timecode results; gate rendering on measured costs.

## 5. Recruiting & career documents

- **GROUNDED:** Jobscan charges $49.95/month or $29.98/month billed quarterly for resume scans, job matching, cover letters, interview practice, and follow-up notes; it says more than 1,000,000 job seekers rely on the product ([Jobscan plans](https://app.jobscan.co/plan)).
- **GROUNDED:** Teal charges $13/week and meters resume bullets at 10 credits and summaries/cover letters at 2 credits while offering resume-to-job matching ([Teal pricing](https://www.tealhq.com/pricing)). Workable starts at $299/month and includes interview kits and scorecards, showing employer-side budget for structured hiring outputs ([Workable pricing](https://www.workable.com/pricing)).
- **REASONED — buyer/frequency:** Active job seekers run per application (5–30 times in a search); career coaches and resume writers run across clients; hiring managers run per open role.

| Workflow | Input → result | Omo price |
|---|---|---:|
| **REASONED — Resume-to-Role Audit** | resume + job description → match map, missing evidence, rewrite priorities | $0.40/run |
| **REASONED — Tailored Resume Draft** | source resume + job description → truthful targeted resume + change log | $0.90/run |
| **REASONED — Cover Letter** | resume + role + motivation → concise letter + fact-verification checklist | $0.35/run |
| **REASONED — Interview Prep Pack** | resume + role + company facts → likely questions, STAR prompts, questions to ask | $0.60/run |
| **REASONED — Structured Interview Kit** | role requirements + level → questions, anchored rubric, note template | $0.75/run |

- **REASONED — creator/affiliate angle:** Career coaches, resume writers, interview educators, university career centers, and recruiting creators have a natural per-application or client referral moment.
- **REASONED — risk:** Personal data, fabricated accomplishments, discriminatory wording, false ATS guarantees, candidate-ranking harm, and job-board terms. Keep results advisory, prohibit invented facts, and do not score or rank people.

## Why these three, not the next alternatives

- **REASONED:** Legal/contracts, finance/accounting, and health/fitness have bounded outputs but a higher review, liability, jurisdiction, and support burden; defer until Omo has expert review and stronger safety controls.
- **REASONED:** General development and data-analysis workflows can command value but depend heavily on repository, runtime, database, and schema context, weakening one-input/one-result reliability.
- **REASONED:** Music/audio generation and full-length video have creator reach but higher rights, compute, storage, and revision risk. Short-form repurposing is the narrower first step.

## Final ranked shortlist

| Rank | Category | Why now | Why pay-per-use fits |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Education** | **GROUNDED/REASONED:** Omo has the audience and live phonics price anchor; paid teacher tools validate the jobs. | **REASONED:** Each lesson asset is bounded, cheap, inspectable, and repeated weekly. |
| 2 | **E-commerce** | **GROUNDED/REASONED:** Describely already validates $0.75/product while photo/ad tools meter credits. | **REASONED:** Every SKU, campaign, review batch, or flow is a natural run with measurable merchant value. |
| 3 | **Real-estate listing media** | **GROUNDED/REASONED:** BoxBrownie already prices the same results from $1.60 to $64 apiece. | **REASONED:** A listing decomposes into several high-value, buyer-approved results without an ongoing relationship. |
| 4 | **Short-form content repurposing** | **GROUNDED/REASONED:** OpusClip and Castmagic prove recurring credit/subscription spend on per-upload results. | **REASONED:** One recording repeatedly becomes a fixed clip, transcript, caption, or content-pack run. |
| 5 | **Recruiting & career documents** | **GROUNDED/REASONED:** Jobscan and Teal show strong burst-period spending on per-application outcomes. | **REASONED:** Job seekers avoid a long subscription and pay only for the applications/interviews that matter. |

## Launch sequence

1. **REASONED:** Validate e-commerce with 30 paid text runs across the three first-ship workflows; track repeat buyer rate, edit rate, refund rate, and contribution margin.
2. **REASONED:** In parallel, recruit supply from one outcome-native creator cohort per category; compensate only on completed referred runs under Omo's existing 95/5 economics.
3. **REASONED:** Open real-estate text results next; hold image activation until a 20-input blind QA set clears fact, disclosure, retry, and margin gates.
4. **REASONED:** Open short-form transcript/timecode results before rendering; measure upload failures and human clip acceptance.
5. **REASONED:** Open career-document results with strict truthfulness/privacy rules and no candidate scoring or hiring recommendation.
