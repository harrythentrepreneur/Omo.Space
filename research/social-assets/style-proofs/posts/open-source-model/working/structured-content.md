- **Audience:** developers and creator-economy buyers evaluating whether Omo is
  trustworthy enough to pay per run.
- **Viewer takeaway:** Omo's open-source model is real and inspectable: free
  skills, MIT, pay only for the run.
- **Platform:** X landscape (16:9, 1600x900, text inside central 84%).
- **Layout:** `hub-spoke` — product/idea in a large central shape, 3-6
  independent spokes.
- **Theme:** `omo-terminal` — cream paper, ASCII banner (pyfiglet), stone box,
  monospace, one orange accent.
- **Headline (on-image):** `$ omo model --open`
- **Central hub text:** `OPEN SOURCE` (large); sub-line `omo.space/skills`.
- **Spokes (exact, sourced):**
  1. `MIT` — everything publishes under the MIT license
  2. `15 free skills` — shipped in the first public publish (commit 104a2b3)
  3. `FREE to download` — the skill contract downloads free
  4. `Pay per run` — the hosted run is the paid product, no subscription
  5. `Readable` — every skill ships its SKILL.md contract in the repo
  6. `1 flagship paid` — the only exclusion: illustrated-decodable-story-maker
- **Visual metaphor:** a terminal window whose output IS the business model;
  spokes radiate from a pine hub like honest log lines.
- **CTA:** `omo.space` (cream pill, pine text) — one orange element: the

- **Sources:** `research/oss-publish-spec.md` (implemented 2026-08-16; first
