# Omo Space launch posts

## 01 — Manifesto

**On-image:** Buy the result. Not another subscription.

AI should end in something useful.

Omo is a marketplace of workflows that turn a clear input into a finished result.

Pay per run. Keep the result.

## 02 — How it works

**On-image:** Pick a workflow. Add your inputs.

No blank chat box. No maze of settings.

Pick the workflow that does the job, give it the inputs it needs, and get the result.

## 03 — Open source

**On-image:** Free to download. Pay to host the run.

The recipe should not be locked away.

Download the skill. Read it. Run it yourself, or let Omo host the execution and pay per run.

## 04 — Marketplace

**On-image:** One marketplace. Many useful outcomes.

The point is not another list of AI tools.

The point is the finished work: books, charts, videos, documents, and the workflows that make them.

## 05 — Brand statement

**On-image:** One prompt in. Finished work out.

Omo is the marketplace for useful AI workflows.

Small tools. Clear inputs. Tangible results.
