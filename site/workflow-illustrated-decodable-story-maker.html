What you give it

  • art_style — Type: choice (choices: 2d, watercolor, paper-cut; required). Example: "2d". What you give it: phonemes.
  • curriculum — Type: choice (choices: general, vic, sg_moe, us_ccss; required). Example: "general". What you give it: story_idea.
  • difficulty_level — Type: choice (choices: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6; required). Example: "2". What you give it: difficulty_level.
  • highlight_text — Type: boolean (required). Example: true. What you give it: language_variant.
  • language_variant — Type: choice (choices: en_au, en_uk, en_us, fr; required). Example: "en_us". What you give it: curriculum.
  • page_count — Type: integer (minimum 7; maximum 21; required). Example: 7. What you give it: page_count.
  • phonemes — Type: array (1–12 items; required). Example: ["ch"]. What you give it: highlight_text.
  • printable — Type: boolean (required). Example: true. What you give it: progressive_highlighting.
  • progressive_highlighting — Type: boolean (required). Example: false. What you give it: printable.
  • story_idea — Type: string (required). Example: "A child helps a chick find its red lunch box.". What you give it: art_style.
