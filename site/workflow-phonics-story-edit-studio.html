Test and source note

The reviewed profile supplies a contract fixture, not a live customer run. It does not identify an independent reviewer or record elapsed time, revision count, or a published evaluation rubric.

Source: packages/phonicsmaker/edit-studio/SKILL.md and packages/skill-to-modal/profiles/phonics-story-edit-studio.json, with pricing status from containers/phonics-story-edit-studio/pricing-report.json.

Limitations and review

  • Owner-authorized source resolution and immutable artifact output are not implemented.
  • The PhonicsMaker compositor, fonts, image inputs, and PDF QA are not packaged for Modal.
  • Optional image regeneration lacks approved provider cost, moderation, and continuity gates.
  • Unavailable until ownership, immutable versioning, and rendering are reviewed.
