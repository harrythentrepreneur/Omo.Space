Omo's bounded workflow is designed to produce one dialect-aware phonics word list, while Twee Pro costs $10.50/mo billed annually and supports broader CEFR-aware ESL lessons and exercises. Omo's catalog currently marks the word-list workflow Coming soon, so it is not yet a buyable alternative.
